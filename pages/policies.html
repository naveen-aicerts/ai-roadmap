Policies

Only policies that appear in the syllabus are listed. Everything else is marked as a placeholder.

Completion standard

A topic is not complete because it has been read. You must be able to explain it and implement it.

Pace

The syllabus recommends teaching in depth, with practical coding and projects, and adjusting the pace to your understanding rather than rushing to meet a deadline.

Scope

The syllabus recommends freezing the topic list and focusing on depth, coding, real projects, debugging, architecture and interview readiness. The aim is a working portfolio of at least three substantial AI systems, deployed and operated.

Engineering judgment

Do not build a multi-agent system for every problem. Know when a simple API call, deterministic workflow, RAG pipeline or single agent is the better architecture.
